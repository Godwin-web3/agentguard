Brand

Bounds, held.

AgentGuard proves that an agent payment stayed inside limits a person signed. The identity says the same thing: two opposing brackets, the bounds of a mandate, hold a solid core, the payment. Quiet, exact, and checkable.

01 · Logo

The mark

A 32-unit grid on a 4-unit module. The brackets are 4 units thick; the core is 12 × 12, centred, a 4-unit gap from each bracket, all inside a 2-unit inset. Built from rectangles only, so it stays sharp at 16 px.

Clear space and size

Keep clear space equal to the core (12 units, ⅜ of the mark's height) on every side. Minimum size: mark 16 px, full logo 96 px wide. Below 24 px use the mark alone.

02 · Color

Ink and cobalt

Neutral ink carries almost everything. Cobalt is reserved for the core of the mark, primary actions and links. Status colors mean exactly one thing each: green is VALID, red is INVALID, amber is held for a human. No gradients.

05 · Voice

Say what is checked

Do

  • "Captured 10.80 USD on order 8FF97705JB819194D after your passkey approved it."
  • "INVALID: amount differs from the signed order."
  • "Sandbox" whenever money isn't real.

Don't

  • "Bank-grade, military-grade, unhackable."
  • Shields, padlocks-with-checkmarks, glowing gradients, emoji.
  • Claim AP2 or PayPal endorsement. AgentGuard is AP2-style and runs on PayPal's public APIs.
