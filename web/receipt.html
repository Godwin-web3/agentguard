Receipt verifier

Verify a receipt in your browser.

The same verifier as the intent-verify CLI, compiled for the browser. It checks the issuer signature against a pinned key set, both passkey signatures, the order against the mandate, RFC 3161 timestamps and the capture. The receipt never leaves this page.
